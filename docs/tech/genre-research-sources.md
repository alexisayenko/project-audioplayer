# Genre research sources

Links used for genre research (genre tree, reference tracks), with how much we trust each. Added 2026-10-24 on the owner's request ("store somewhere this one").

Policy: Rate Your Music (RYM) is the reference for subgenres; Discogs styles and Musicmap also attest ([genre-terminology.md](genre-terminology.md#source-policy-for-new-genres)). Everything else is corroborating only and never the sole basis for a genre or a pick. Claude cannot fetch RYM, Discogs or AllMusic (HTTP 403): the owner pastes the page text or a screenshot.

Kinds: reference (can attest a genre), corroborating (community or encyclopedic, supports a claim), weak (commercial or single-author, role or colour only).
