## goa trance

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goa_trance), [Rate Your Music](https://rateyourmusic.com/genre/goa-trance/) (also listed there as "Goa 604"; 1,884 releases per Alex's paste). Findings below are paraphrased.

- Origin: early 1990s, the Goa scene in India; forefather of psytrance.
- Sound: about 135-150 BPM, rapid arpeggios, psychedelic and often South Asian-inspired melodies, long tracks.
- Influences: EBM, acid house, new beat (a "proto-goa" wave of mostly new beat tracks), acid trance, classic trance.
- DJs who shaped the scene: DJ Laurent, Ray Castle, Goa Gil, Fred Disko.
- Compilations: "Dragonfly: Project II Trance" is essential; Paul Oakenfold's "Goa Mix" popularised the sound in the UK.
- Labels: Dragonfly, Flying Rhino, TIP, Blue Room Released (London), Harthouse (Sven Väth, Germany).
- Scenes: Japan and Israel (Astral Projection; later Nitzhonot).
- Artists named: X-Dream, Hallucinogen, Etnica, Juno Reactor, Astral Projection.
- Late 1990s: the sound turned more metallic and led to full-on, progressive and dark psytrance (end of the goa era).
- 2000s "new school goa" revival: Suntrip Records, Goasia, Artha, Morphic Resonance.

### Musicmap

Source: [Musicmap](https://musicmap.info/) (genre map; the genre sits in its Trance branch, start year 1993; no deep link). Findings below are paraphrased.

- Goa trance and psytrance are treated as two technically different but very similar genres.
- Origin: Goa, India, among hippies who wanted psychedelic music as house and techno took over; trance suited psychedelic drug experiences.
- Influences came along the hippie trail: Ibiza, the Middle East, dream and ambient trance, Indian raga.
- Sound: dense, many-layered dance music with psychedelic effects, intricate melodies and very clean, punchy kicks.
- Subculture: parties and decoration teams; stayed underground but popular in Israel, Brazil, India, Finland, Germany.
- Subgenres named: Nitzhonot (Israeli, fusion with epic trance), Full-On, Suomisaundi (Finnish, minimal, rave-like), Dark Psy, Morning Trance (sunrise outdoor sets), Old Skool Goa Trance.
- On the map: roots in classic and acid trance, Ibiza and dream trance / house, and EBM; near progressive trance and ambient dance.

### Compare with the other sources

- Dates: Wikipedia and Rate Your Music say early 1990s; Musicmap says 1993 (consistent).
- Roots: Musicmap lists EBM, classic/acid trance and Ibiza/dream trance; Rate Your Music names EBM, acid house, new beat, acid trance and classic trance. The overlaps (EBM, acid trance, classic trance) are the best-supported claims.
- Only Musicmap adds Ibiza/dream trance and Indian raga; only Rate Your Music adds acid house and new beat.
