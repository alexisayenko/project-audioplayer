## Accepted iOS playback target (not implemented)

Library remains the collection, separate from the current playing queue.
Under [ADR-0043](../tech/decisions.md#adr-0043-persistent-queue-and-ephemeral-visible-song-audition),
Library and Song Details Play/Pause will audition one visible song independently
of queue playback, with both audible simultaneously; explicit Add to queue
changes membership separately. Leaving the owning surface, scrolling the row
away, locking or backgrounding stops audition. The dock remains bound to the
queue. [Requirements and unresolved existing queue commands](playback.md#two-independent-playback-contexts-adr-0043).

The LIB rules below describe the existing **web** implementation; its shared
player controls are not evidence of an implemented independent audition.
iOS implementation status and recent row/menu layout are in
[ios.md](ios.md) and [Library v5](../ui-ux/library-v5.md).

- LIB-019a MAY (changed 2026-10-24, task-0073): in the song card the BPM badge is a toggle button (`aria-pressed`, tooltip, focus ring, hover border). Off (default) it shows the stored BPM in the neutral colour; on, it turns azure (`--accent-ink`) and shows the tempo measured from the playing audio as measured, with one decimal up to and including 200 BPM (`127.4`) and a whole number above (a 1000 BPM track shows `1000`, the badge grows to fit four digits at 375px; owner request 2026-10-24, FK1; `liveBpmLabel` in `bpm-label.js`; the stored value and the off state stay whole numbers); `~<stored>` is shown only while the song plays and the detector has not locked (about 4 s after play, seek or song change, and as long as it finds no steady pulse or the audio graph is not running). With the toggle on, the badge shows `- - - BPM` (aria-label "Live BPM, nothing playing") instead of `~<stored>` on the card of a song that is not playing now (nothing playing, paused, or another song current) and while the audio is silent (RMS under 0.002 for 1.5 s; the detector is cleared). The state is global and stored in `localStorage` key `ap.liveBpm`. Turning it on (and every play while on) builds the audio graph the visualizer uses (`audio-graph.js`), so the toggle works without opening the visualizer; before that fix the analyser did not exist and the badge showed `~<stored>` for every song. Detection: PCM from the analyser's time-domain buffer every 40 ms (no second `MediaElementSource`), a 400 Hz onset envelope (rise of log energy in three bands: below 150 Hz, 150 to 1500 Hz, above), autocorrelation over a 6 s window (tapered) and over its newest 3 s (owner request 2026-10-24: the badge lagged 6 to 7 s behind a tempo change; the 6 s window, the 3-value median and the 1 s cadence added up): when the two results agree (within 3%, or an octave apart, which the short window misreads on slow songs) the 6 s one is shown, otherwise the 3 s one (a tempo change); search range 30 to 1200 BPM, the shortest lag within 5% of the strongest peak wins (the pulse, not a bar), the stored BPM or the usual 60 to 200 range break only near ties (within 15%, never a clearly stronger candidate, and never above 200 BPM), the period refined from the multiples of the peak, the median of the last 3 half-second estimates is shown (step changes of the synthetic 60 to 1000 BPM file are followed in 2 to 2.5 s, 5 s for 500 to 800 BPM; before 4 to 6 s); steady noise, tones and pads (no onsets) give no value. It resets on song change, seek, resume and a gap in sampling (hidden tab), and runs only while on and playing (`live-bpm.js`, `live-bpm-ctl.js`). Status: implemented (browser-checked on synthetic fixtures, not on real songs)
