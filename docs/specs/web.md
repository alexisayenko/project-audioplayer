Code: `web/js/*.js` (module map: [architecture.md](../tech/architecture.md#web-ui-web); the sign-in is in `settings.js`, `api.js`, `app.js`; the player in `player.js` and the pure `queue-logic`, `scrub`, `dl-queue`, `dl-progress`, `playable`, `reveal`, `sidebar-state` modules; the Genres map in `genres.js`, `genre-*.js`; the visualizer terrain modes in `viz-synth-*.js`, `viz-terrain-math.js`, `viz-terrain-gl.js` and `viz-modes.js`, wired into the mode switch, WEB-064, VIZ-040); server side in
[api.md](api.md) (API-020..024) and ADR-0006. Status values reflect code read
on 2026-10-13 (the web is a catalog UI: it saves song entries and fetches audio only on demand, WEB-034 and WEB-036); the session sign-in flow was seen live on 2026-10-14 (server journal: 6 successful `POST /auth/session`, 5 user profiles); which provider was used is not recorded.

- WEB-112 MUST (owner decision 2026-10-19): the default Graph layout minimises crossings of parent and extra-parent links; related links do not count. A deterministic radial sector layout seeds positions (`web/js/genre-tree-layout.js`): family roots sit on a ring, each genre takes the sector of its canonical primary parent in proportion to subtree size, and siblings are ordered by the barycenter of their other parents. The primary-parent tree has zero crossings by construction. A seeded annealing pass over node positions then reduces crossings (single circles or a circle with its leaf children as one block; 80 percent of moves target nodes incident to crossings; a heavy overlap penalty and weak pull toward the seed keep the layout legible). It runs for 30000 moves in time slices of at most 6 ms per frame while circles glide to their new rest positions (`genre-layout.js`, `genre-crossing-anneal.js`, `genre-crossing.js`). The simulation preserves the result with weak repulsion, collisions, springs at layout distances and a rest-position spring; related and fusion-hidden links neither count nor pull strongly. Dragging stays free and the dropped position becomes the new rest position. "Reset layout" re-runs the automatic layout; under reduced motion the layout is computed at once and does not animate.

  Historical benchmarks were measured on a smaller Rock-hierarchy dataset: force baseline 560 crossings to 154 (73 percent fewer) with Fusion on, and 405 to 115 (72 percent fewer) with it off. The current full vocabulary has 14 roots and different cross-link density, so the historical 65 percent target is not comparable. Current acceptance requires zero primary-parent crossings in the seed and at least 25 percent fewer total crossings than the force baseline after annealing, with the result also beating the sector seed; both Fusion settings, determinism and time bounds are tested in `web/js/genre-layout.test.mjs`. `genre-force-view.test.mjs` covers interaction.
