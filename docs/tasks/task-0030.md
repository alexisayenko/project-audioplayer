# Synthwave terrain visualizer mode

A fourth visualizer mode `synthwave` ("Synthwave terrain") next to Mirrored spectrum, Ring and Terrain: one mode at a time, same switcher, keys and settings system. Visually a restyle of the WebGL Terrain. Requirements: [visualizer.md](../specs/visualizer.md#synthwave-terrain-planned) (VIZ-080 to VIZ-091). Web only.

## Dependency

Builds on the existing Terrain code and reuses where sensible: `viz-terrain-math.js`, `viz-terrain-gl.js`, `viz-lookahead.js` (timeline, future rows), `viz-beats.js`, LOD tiers, `viz-pacing.js`, `viz-modes.js`, `viz-panel.js`. Changes to Terrain itself must not alter its liked baselines (`terrain-liked-2`).

## Components and adjustable properties

1. Terrain surface and grid: style switchable between square grid and triangle mesh (decided), speed, line and fill colours, glow, thickness, cell size.
2. Peaks: shape (flat plain to low-poly mountains to sharp spikes), wireframe or filled faces, valley, horizon silhouette, colours, fading speed, future silhouette (faint outline of the upcoming terrain ahead of the viewer; visibility and distance TBD).
3. Sun: colours (gradient allowed), radius, slices in the lower half only (start, count, thickness growing toward the bottom, smooth or stepped).
4. Sun haze: ring and horizon band, colour, radius, softness, movement.
5. Sun reflection on the ground: streak or blurred copy, colour or gradient, opacity, fade, wet-floor sheen.
6. Horizon line: colour, thickness, glow, optional colour band, hard or soft.
7. Background space: sky gradient, number and size of stars, vignette.
