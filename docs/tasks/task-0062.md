# Info popovers on the parts of the song card title line

Artist facts are a stub until a real source is decided (decision AL).

In the song card's plain text line `Artist - Title (Version)` (the line with the copy icon, LIB-155) every artist, the title and the version are separate buttons. A click, tap or Enter/Space opens a small popover anchored to the part; hover only underlines.

- Artist: Country, Genres, Years of activity, link "Filter library by this artist".
- Title: Year of release, Albums, Genres (the song's own data).
- Version: Year of release (version year, else the song's), Albums, Genres (the song's).

Rows without data are omitted; a popover with no rows says "No details yet".
