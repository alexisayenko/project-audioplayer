## Scope of step 1

- Synth controls, in this order: **Frequency** in Hz (first), **Harmonics** Hn (1 to 32, H1 = the fundamental), **Decay** across harmonics in dB per octave (0 to -24; -6 dB/oct = amplitude 1/n), **Play/Stop** with short fades (no clicks). A steady tone, no envelope.
- Waveform view: X time, Y amplitude, 3 to 4 cycles; optionally the individual harmonics as faint lines under the sum.
- Spectrum view: X frequency (log), Y dB, one bar per harmonic H1..Hn, each labelled with its Hz.
- Spectrogram view: X time (scrolls, about 10 s), Y frequency (log), colour = level.
- A "What are harmonics?" text block on the page: fundamental (H1), harmonics, overtones, partials, timbre.

## Labelling and terminology

- Bars and labels read H1, H2, ... with the Hz value; H1 is called "Fundamental (H1)". "1f", "2f" appear only in explainer text.
- Overtone n = harmonic n+1 (the first overtone is H2). Partial is any component of a sound, harmonic or not; a harmonic is a partial at an exact multiple of H1.

## Open questions (recommendations, not decisions)

- A. Frequency range? Recommended: 20 to 2000 Hz, default 220 Hz; harmonics above half the sample rate are dropped, not aliased.
- B. Where do the spectrum levels come from? Recommended: an `AnalyserNode` on the real synth signal, so the same views can later show other sources ([task-0108](task-0108.md)).
- C. Output level and the song: Recommended: a fixed safe master gain; Play pauses a playing song and does not mix.
- D. Default Hn and decay? Decided by the owner on 2026-10-24: 10 harmonics, -12 dB/oct (step 1 first shipped with 8 and -6).
