## Facts to keep

- Audio session: category `.playAndRecord`, mode `.measurement`, options `[.mixWithOthers, .defaultToSpeaker]`. Another app's music keeps playing and the microphone hears the phone speaker.
- Limit: the microphone hears the phone speaker only, not headphones or Bluetooth. iOS cannot capture another app's internal audio without a screen-recording broadcast extension.
- `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` in the app's Info.plist (`ios/project.yml`).
- A Swift port of the web Live BPM engine (`LiveBPM.swift`), so both platforms detect the same way.
- The screen has no home yet: iOS has no Studio. Undecided: a drawer entry or a row in Settings.
- Editing `ios/` was permitted for the first attempt only (stopped); ask again.

## Mac app

The Mac app shows the web GUI, so the web Tools tab needs the microphone there: the entitlement `com.apple.security.device.audio-input`, `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, and a `WKUIDelegate` media-capture permission handler in the web view. Without the handler the Tools tab cannot open the microphone in the Mac app.
