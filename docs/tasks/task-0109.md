# Studio Tools: harmonics lab extras

> Alex (2026-10-24): step 1 is only the synth; "the rest: create follow-up tickets". Then, after hearing it: the steady tone "sounds like a chainsaw, never like a piano"; he chose FW2, a continuous piano-like decay mode (not a one-shot Strike button), and defaults of 10 harmonics at -12 dB/oct.

Follow-up of [task-0106](task-0106.md). The time-decay envelope (2026-10-24, WEB-184 and WEB-185, [ADR-0048](../tech/decisions.md)) and the multiple detuned strings (WEB-186 and WEB-187) are built; the rest is not.

## Done

- Time-decay envelope, as the "Piano-like decay" checkbox (off by default; off is the steady tone): each harmonic fades exponentially with `tau_n = tau1 / n^0.8` after a 5 ms attack, "Fade time (H1)" 0.2 to 5 s (default 1.2), "Repeat every" 1 to 6 s (default 3); the tone re-strikes by itself, scheduled on the `AudioParam` timeline; the three views show the real signal.
- Defaults: 10 harmonics, -12 dB/oct (was 8 and -6).
- Piano-style strings (owner decision FX1, 2026-10-24; WEB-186, WEB-187, ADR-0048 amended): "Strings per note" 1, 2 or 3 (default 1, the old sound; widened to 1 to 8 on 2026-10-24, owner decision GH2, WEB-193, evenly spread offsets, at most 256 oscillators, built, not deployed) and "Detune between strings (cents)" 0 to 5 (default 2, shown from 2 strings); each string is its own stack of harmonics with the same roll-off and decay, offset symmetrically in cents; gains divided by the string count so the sum stays at or below 0.3; live "H1 beats about X Hz" readout, the explainer line and a beats hint under the waveform. Checked in a local Chromium page (96 oscillators, 439.49 / 440 / 440.51 Hz at 440 Hz and 2 cents, a swell of the analyser peak at the beat rate); deployed 8c7ede1, sound not heard.

- "Piano realism" group (owner decision GB3, 2026-10-24, "still not like a real piano"; WEB-189, ADR-0048 amended), shown only while the piano-like decay is on, four switches on by default: hammer thump (noise burst, level -48 to -12 dB, default -24), two-stage decay (0.75 at `tau_n` plus 0.25 at `4 tau_n`), inharmonicity B 0 to 0.002 (default 0.0004, `f_n = n f sqrt(1 + B n^2)`, labels show the real Hz) and hammer position 1/12 to 1/4 (default 1/8, amplitude times `|sin(n pi p)|`, floor 0.03). Changes apply at the next strike. Checked in a local Chromium page with hooks (H10 at 4487.14 Hz, H8 about 25 dB down, thump bins 15 to 30 dB up, a longer tail); built, not deployed at the time of writing; sound not heard.

## Remains (all optional controls on the lab)

- Odd/even toggles: all, odd only, even only (H1 always sounds).
- Register-dependent B and strings count by note (bass notes have more inharmonicity and fewer strings; today B is one slider and the string count is manual).
- A4 reference from 415 to 466 Hz (default 440), used by the note picker and by the [note detector](task-0107.md).
- Note or keyboard picker: choose a note (name and octave) or press an on-screen keyboard key and the Frequency field follows.
- Presets: sine, triangle, sawtooth, square, piano-like. Each sets Hn, decay, odd/even and, for piano-like, B and the envelope (the envelope itself now exists as the checkbox, the realism group sets B).
