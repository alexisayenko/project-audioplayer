# Genre card: an infobox for every genre

> Alex (GC2, 2026-10-23): the genre details live in a popup infobox card like the song card (opens on top of wherever you are), not a separate page and not tabs.

Spec: GEN-089..GEN-097 in [genres.md](../specs/genres.md). Related: [task-0093](task-0093.md) (reference tracks), [task-0076](task-0076.md) (history).

## Decisions

- A popup card, same pattern as the song card: no page, no route, no tabs.
- The genre map screen keeps its settings on the right and shows only a short preview in its side panel: name, one-line description, "Open card".
- Guests: not applicable (the public view is off).

## Entry points

- Genre map: click on a genre and the info button.
- Genre chips on songs: song card, Library rows, Edit dialog.
- Statistics > Genres table; the reference-tracks list.
- Inside another genre card: related, parent, subgenre and group chips; the card swaps to that genre with a back arrow.

## Sections, top to bottom

1. Header: colour dot, name, level, fusion marker, synonyms.
2. Description (own-words note) and source links (Wikipedia, Rate Your Music, Apple Music, Discogs).
3. Where it sits: Parents, Subgenres, Groups, Related as clickable chips.
4. Reference tracks: the task-0093 list (play buttons, role badges, source counts; owner edit link).
5. History, "what came out of what": placeholder until task-0076.
6. Your library: song count, total listened time, top tracks in the genre.
7. Actions: "View in Library" (filters to the genre), "Show on map", owner only "Edit reference tracks".

## Behaviour

- Loaded on demand like the song card (lazy tier-2).
- Escape closes; focus trap and return focus.
- Back arrow walks a stack of visited genres inside the card.
- Phones: full-width sheet.

## Phases

1. The card with sections 1-4 and the map preview panel.
2. Sections 6-7 and the entry points from songs, Library, Edit dialog and Statistics.
3. History (section 5) once task-0076 exists.

None.
