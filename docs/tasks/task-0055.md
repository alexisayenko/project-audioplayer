# Genre parents are equal (no primary parent)

> Alex (BY1, BX): in the genre vocabulary all parents are equal; there is no primary parent.

## Decision

[ADR-0037](../tech/decisions.md): `parents` is an unordered set. A genre belongs to every family it descends from and a song counts in each of them. The level is per path; where one number is needed it is the shortest path depth to a family. The depth cap of three levels is lifted (sanity bound 12). The interface nests a genre under each of its parents and draws it once with a line to each parent; a genre of several families is a pie of equal segments in the family colours. The Electronic family circle is not drawn on the map.

## Consequences

- Data: the `family` override is gone; disco (Funk / Soul and Electronic) passes both families to its children and post-disco sits under disco; owner edits of the same day: psytrance under trance, eurodance (house, euro disco, Pop), darkwave (Electronic, post-punk, new wave), synthpop (Electronic, Pop, new wave), industrial and ethereal removed.
- Server: `genre_vocab` (families, shortest-path level, validation), `genre_suggest`, `genre_filter.is_electronic`, `genre_graph` nodes; payload gains `families`, old keys stay.
- Web: `genre-levels.js` tree (parents, fams, outline rows), radial and graph renderers, pie fill helpers, hidden family circles.
- Specs: GEN-070 to GEN-072 and amendments; CHANGELOG, genre-levels.md.
