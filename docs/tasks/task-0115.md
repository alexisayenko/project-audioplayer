## Physics

- Sound comes from a vibrating column of air in a pipe.
- An open pipe (flute-like) supports all harmonics: H1, H2, H3, and so on.
- A closed pipe (clarinet-like) supports only the odd harmonics: H1, H3, H5.
- End correction: the air vibrates slightly beyond the open end, so the pipe sounds lower than its geometric length suggests.

## Controls to build

- Pipe length or frequency (one drives the other).
- Open or closed pipe.
- Blowing noise level (breath noise mixed with the tone).
- Overblowing (the next register: an octave for open pipes, a twelfth for closed ones).
