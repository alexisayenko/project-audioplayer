# Live BPM on the song card: the BPM badge is a toggle

> Alex: make the BPM badge on the song card a toggle button; on, it turns azure and shows the BPM measured live while the song plays.

## Behaviour

- The badge in the song card details (LIB-019) is a button with `aria-pressed`. Off (default): the stored BPM, neutral colour. On: azure (`--accent-ink`) and the live BPM, e.g. `141`; until the detector has locked (about 5 to 10 s after play, seek or song change) it shows the stored value with a `~` prefix.
- The state is global, stored in `localStorage` key `ap.liveBpm` (`1` / `0`, works without storage).
- Detection taps the visualizer's analyser (`audio-graph.js`, no second `MediaElementSource`): low-band (40-160 Hz) energy per frame, onsets by energy flux with an adaptive threshold, tempo from the pairwise inter-onset intervals over a sliding 10 s window, constrained to 60-200 BPM; the stored BPM resolves half/double errors (within 4%). Display updates about once a second.
- Sampling runs only while the toggle is on and the song is playing; it resets on song change, seek and resume. The live value is shown only on the card of the song that is playing.

## Code

`web/js/live-bpm.js` (pure detector), `web/js/live-bpm-ctl.js` (sampling, persistence), `web/js/song-card.js` (badge), `web/style.css` (`.bpm-toggle`). Tests: `live-bpm.test.mjs`, `live-bpm-ctl.test.mjs`, `song-card-live-bpm.test.mjs`.
