# Live BPM on the song card: the BPM badge is a toggle

> Alex: make the BPM badge on the song card a toggle button; on, it turns azure and shows the BPM measured live while the song plays.

## Behaviour

- The badge in the song card details (LIB-019) is a button with `aria-pressed`. Off (default): the stored BPM, neutral colour. On: azure (`--accent-ink`) and the live BPM, e.g. `141`; until the detector has locked (about 5 to 10 s after play, seek or song change) it shows the stored value with a `~` prefix.
- The state is global, stored in `localStorage` key `ap.liveBpm` (`1` / `0`, works without storage).
- Detection taps the visualizer's analyser (`audio-graph.js`, no second `MediaElementSource`): the time-domain PCM of the analyser buffer every 40 ms, an onset envelope at 400 Hz (rise of log energy in three bands), autocorrelation over a 6 s tapered window and, for the newest 3 s, a second one that wins when it disagrees with the 6 s one (not an octave apart; 2026-10-24, tempo changes show after about 2 s instead of 4 to 6 s), search range 30 to 1200 BPM (changed 2026-10-24; it was 60 to 200 over a 10 s window, which folded a 1000 BPM track to 127), the shortest lag within 5% of the strongest peak wins, the stored BPM and the usual 60 to 200 range only break near ties (15%), the median of the last 3 estimates is shown as measured with one decimal up to 200 BPM and as a whole number above (2026-10-24). Display updates every 0.5 s. With the toggle on and nothing playing (paused, another song current, or silent audio) the badge shows `- - - BPM`; `~<stored>` only while the song plays and has no reading yet (2026-10-24).
- The toggle builds the audio graph when it is turned on and on every play while on (changed 2026-10-24). Before, the analyser existed only after the visualizer, Fade or loop had built the graph, so on a fresh page the detector never got a sample and the badge showed `~<stored>` for every song.
- Sampling runs only while the toggle is on and the song is playing; it resets on song change, seek, resume and after a gap in sampling longer than the analyser buffer (a hidden tab). The live value is shown only on the card of the song that is playing.

## Code

`web/js/live-bpm.js` (pure detector: onset envelope, autocorrelation, tempo choice), `web/js/live-bpm-synth.mjs` (test signal generator), `web/js/live-bpm-ctl.js` (sampling, persistence), `web/js/song-card.js` (badge), `web/style.css` (`.bpm-toggle`). Tests: `live-bpm.test.mjs`, `live-bpm-ctl.test.mjs`, `song-card-live-bpm.test.mjs`.
