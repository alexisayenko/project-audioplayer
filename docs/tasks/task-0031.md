Owner look check on real playback pending.

Eight one-click looks for the Synthwave mode ([task-0030](task-0030.md)), taken from Alex's reference images. Requirements: [visualizer.md](../specs/visualizer.md#synthwave-presets) (VIZ-092 to VIZ-097). Web only. The existing Terrain mode and its `terrain-liked-2` baseline are not touched.

## Decisions

- A "Presets" section sits above "Fine tune" in the Synthwave panel; thumbnails, 2 to 3 per row.
- Picking a preset overwrites all Fine tune values; no undo, no user-saved presets.
- Thumbnails are screenshots of our renderer, not the reference images (those stay local: [synthwave-references](../brand/synthwave-references/README.md)).
- Presets 7 and 8 take only colours and objects from their references.
- Where the settings schema cannot express a detail, use the nearest settings and list the gap below; the renderer is extended only where the schema already supports it.

## Known gaps

Visual (from the thumbnail review):

- Relief is asymmetric: strongest on the bass side, the left wall is weaker.
- blue-valley: white faces wash out near the horizon, with far-LOD aliasing speckle.
- neon-sea: the reflection is faint and the sea is near-black (needs a renderer change).
- violet-plain: horizon peaks are low and not clearly blue-edged.

Schema (not expressible today):

- No truly flat terrain.
- No grid-off switch.
- One sun gradient axis only.
- No stacked stripe horizon band.
- No foreground noise.
- No per-star colours.
- No upper-right-only haze.
