iOS: in TestFlight build 20261032 uploaded 2026-10-24; not checked on a device.

in the expanded iOS playback view. The row appears only when the song has
genres, can scroll horizontally when needed, and does not introduce genre
selection or filtering behavior. The desktop web player's mouse-oriented UX is
out of scope.
