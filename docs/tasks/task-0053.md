# Fade out and fade in between songs (crossfade)

> Alex: fadeout/up song to the next.

## Why

Songs in the queue currently cut hard from one to the next. A short fade out of the ending song and fade up of the next one makes the queue feel like a set.

## Proposed behaviour

- Settings: "Fade between songs" with a length in seconds (off, 1 s, 2 s, 3 s, 5 s; default off) or as a percentage of the song (for example 3%, 5%, 10%), so a long track with a long built-in intro or outro (an 8-minute mix) fades over a proportionally long stretch; an optional cap in seconds keeps short songs sane and a mode: sequential (fade out, then fade up) or overlap (crossfade, both audible).
- Applies to the automatic advance and to Next; a manual jump by tapping a row uses a short fade out only. Pause/resume keep their own tiny ramp to avoid clicks.
- Web: two audio elements (or Web Audio gain nodes) driven by the player; iOS: two AVPlayers or volume ramps. Playback and background reliability come first: no change to lock-screen controls, and if the next file is not ready the fade is skipped.
- Works with the trim (task-0052) and clip (task-0045) windows: the fade starts relative to the effective end.

## Decisions (2026-10-21, Alex)

- Changed later the same day: a PERCENT of the song length, not seconds (a fixed 3 s is pointless on a 10-minute track with a long intro or outro; about 8 to 10 % is right on a 3-minute song). Settings page only (no Studio control). Fade in and fade out of single songs only; NO crossfade or sequential/overlap mode for now (the sections above stay as the later scope).
- Web first: "Fade in" and "Fade out" in Settings > Playback, each 0 to 15 % in 1 % steps, default Off (0), with a live hint for the loaded song ("≈ 18 s on this song"). Fade seconds = percent of the duration, held to 60 s (a 30-minute mix at 15 % would fade for 4.5 minutes) and to half of the song; no minimum in seconds (a 20 s clip at 10 % fades 2 s); unknown duration means no fade. Stored as percent in `ap2.fadePct.in` and `ap2.fadePct.out` (the earlier seconds keys are not read). Spec PLY-180 to PLY-184 in [playback.md](../specs/playback.md). Built, not run, not heard; iOS not started.
- Alex said "cross-fade 1 minute even" about the fade length (so the 60 s ceiling). A real crossfade (two songs overlapping) is still not built; it stays a later step and needs a second audio stream.
