# iOS UI/UX team loop: Library, dock player, Now playing, Song Details

An autonomous audit, fix, re-audit loop over the iOS UI/UX of Library Songs, Library Albums, the dock (mini) player, Now playing and Song Details (song infobox).

## Scope

Layout, spacing, hierarchy, alignment, touch targets, consistency with the design system and [ux-preferences](../product/ux-preferences.md), clarity of labels and states, Dynamic Type and dark mode robustness. Files under `ios/Sources` only (plus `ios/Tests` when a test asserts an old in-scope value).

## Out of scope

- New features.
- The Library v5 redesign ([library-v5](../ui-ux/library-v5.md), design-only): no controls-line rework, no Era chip, no Play matched, no collapse-on-scroll.
- Any change to playback, queue, downloads or offline behaviour.
- Web, server and `ios/MacSources`; other screens (Settings, Tools, Playlists, Statistics, drawer).
- Hand-editing `ios/Sources/DesignTokens.swift`; colours, sizes or radii outside the design tokens.

## Invariants

- Playback, queue, downloads and offline use work as before.
- Schemes `AudioPlayer` and `ElectrophoneMac` build; unit tests pass.
- HIG fixes IOS-120 to IOS-129 still hold (back swipe, drawer rows, Dynamic Type up to AX5, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, confirmations).

## Status

Loop stopped after round 8 (no progress). Criteria not met: 1 P1, visual sanity not OK ([audit-r8](../ui-ux/ios-loop/audit-r8.md)). Rules: IOS-130 in [ios spec](../specs/ios.md).

## Remaining

- L-H1 (P1): album track rows cut titles; drop or shrink the repeated thumbnail, shrink the trailing group, allow two title lines.
- L-H2, L-H3 (P2): Library rows with a play count lose the year; titles cut with empty space; move the count under the duration.
- L-H4, P-H1 (P2): at AX5 the pinned block and dock leave room for one row; shrink the dock, one-line title at accessibility sizes.
