# Studio Tools: spectrum analyzer tool

> Alex (2026-10-24): step 1 is only the synth; "the rest: create follow-up tickets".

Follow-up of [task-0106](task-0106.md). Nothing is built.

## Scope

- A band display: **32 logarithmic bands** from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, FFT size 4096, level range -90 to 0 dBFS.
- **Peak caps** per band that hold and then fall; a **Freeze** button that stops the display and keeps the last frame.
- Frequency labels at 100 Hz, 1 kHz and 10 kHz; a dB scale on the side.
- Works on the sources of [task-0108](task-0108.md) (Synth, Microphone, Player) once they exist; until then on the synth and the microphone.

## Relations

- The web visualizer's "Mirrored spectrum" mode ([visualizer.md](../specs/visualizer.md), [task-0023](task-0023.md)) already maps analyser bins to bars for the playing song. This tool is a measuring view (labelled axes, dBFS, peaks, freeze), the visualizer is decoration. Recommended: share the bin-to-band mapping code, keep separate drawing.
- The harmonics lab spectrum view ([task-0106](task-0106.md)) shows one bar per harmonic; this tool shows all frequencies in fixed bands.
- Part of the Studio Analyze tab, with Live BPM (being built, 2026-10-24).

## Open questions (recommendations, not decisions)

- A. Band spacing: equal in log frequency, or ISO third-octave centres? Recommended: equal log spacing, 32 bands.
- B. Smoothing and peak fall time? Recommended: light smoothing, peak caps fall about 20 dB per second.
- C. FFT 4096 gives about 10.8 Hz bins at 44.1 kHz, so the lowest bands hold fewer than one bin. Recommended: interpolate bins into those bands and note it in the spec.
- D. Pause drawing when the tab is hidden? Recommended: yes.

## Phases

1. 🔲 Spec after A to D.
2. 🔲 Band mapping as a pure function (tests with a synthetic spectrum).
3. 🔲 Canvas view with peaks, freeze and labels.
