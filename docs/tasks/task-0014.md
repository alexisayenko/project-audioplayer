# P9: Import Shazam history

First step: verify the facts below against current Apple/vendor docs before building; they come from memory and are unverified.

Research first: Shazam's library can reach Apple Music as the playlist "My Shazam Tracks", and Apple offers a privacy data export; which of the two exists today, what it contains (title, artist, date, Shazam or Apple Music id) and its format are unchecked.

Scope after research: decide the format, then a one-off import script or endpoint that turns the history into metadata-only records (`applemusic-<id>` where known), skipping duplicates. Done when: the research outcome is written here, the chosen import runs once on the owner's history without audio downloads, and CAT-047..048 are implemented. Stays `proposal` until the research is done.

Depends on: task-0006, task-0012. Specs: [`catalog.md`](../specs/catalog.md).
Gate: ADR-0010 must be Accepted before code starts.
