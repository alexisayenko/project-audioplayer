# Restructure Electronic: forms at level 1, Electronic and EDM as groups

> Alex, AN1: start the restructure. Electronic is a medium ("I made a house track"), not a genre.

## Goal

Make the Electronic family stop being a node of the tree and the `electronic` pill stop being a genre of songs. The Electronic forms (house, techno, trance, drum and bass, breakbeat, ambient, eurodance, synthwave, experimental, and uk garage if CG says yes) become level 1 genres, like Rock. Electronic and EDM become groups: never a parent in the chain, never assigned to a song. "Electronic" survives as a medium derived from the tree. Model: [genre-terminology.md](../tech/genre-terminology.md) and ADR-0038 in [decisions.md](../tech/decisions.md); mapping of the 146 genres: [genre-electronic-forms.md](../tech/genre-electronic-forms.md) (PROPOSAL, not applied).

Nothing is built. This task is documentation of a plan; the analysis was read-only (live `GET /library`, 422 songs, and `GET /genres/vocabulary`, 545 rows, 2026-10-20).

## Decisions (owner, 2026-10-20)

- Axes Form / Medium / Purpose / Tradition (+ personal Mood); modifiers (renamed from flavours; kinds sound, mood, scene); groups are never assigned to songs: Electronic, EDM (a purpose set: electronic dance music), Latin, World.
- Electronic is NOT a genre. AN1: start the restructure.
- Level 1 forms: house, techno, trance, drum and bass (separate from breakbeat), breakbeat, ambient, eurodance, synthwave, experimental (CB1). `industrial` is a modifier of kind sound, not a node. uk garage is proposed (CG pending).
- synthpop is NOT level 1 (CC1): parents Pop and new wave (Rock), `related` disco, electronic by medium. Synonyms `techno-pop`, `technopop` (CD1); techno-pop is not techno.
- jungle: parents breakbeat and drum and bass, `related` reggae (BZ1).
- `bass music` is not a form (it names a sound or medium, CA); its genres are redistributed (see the mapping doc; the placement is pending as CG).
- hardcore: the bare word is a sound modifier, the node is `hardcore techno`, no synonym (AX1, implemented, not deployed). `epic` removed as a genre.
- witch house stays under experimental (BV and BU are defaults, unconfirmed); BT: no related link to house.
- Modifier kinds: dark, deep, uplifting are mood. Depth is computed, no "subgenre" or "microgenre" names (AD, unconfirmed).

## Design points (from the analysis plan)

1. **Level-1 node = the genre itself** (like Rock, Jazz, Hip Hop). `House` the subfamily and `house` the genre have the same key; the genre entries `house`, `techno`, `trance` move to the families block, their children keep the same parent text and rise one level. The node needs `related` and `synonyms` support (today the block is `{name, synonyms}`).
2. **`groups` block** in `genre_levels.json`: `{"Electronic": {"order", "synonyms", "members"}}`, and likewise EDM, Latin, World. A group is never in `parents`, never a family, never a row in `genres`, never assignable, has no song count of its own. Keeping Electronic as a parent would put every genre one level deeper and make it a family again. `group` is a set of nodes or of genres, so EDM (a flag over dance genres) and Latin/World fit without another model change.
3. **`electronic` is non-canonical but NOT in `REMOVED_GENRES`/`DROP`.** Those lists are applied by `library.clean_tag` on every write and load, for every user, silently, with no backup and no "never the last genre" guard: adding `electronic` would strip 170 songs at once and leave 7 with no genre. Use a generic migration pass instead (T2).
4. **Aliases that pointed at `electronic`** (dance & edm, club, electronica, indietronica, alternative dance, rave; `genre_filter.ALIASES`) move to DROP/`REMOVED_GENRES` like epic and lo-fi (TAG-081 needs canonical targets); their tags are already folded and none is left in live data. `is_electronic` becomes a group test.
5. **Palette via shades.** Tokens have 16 slots, 12 used; the eight old subfamily nodes cannot all get slots. Forms use Electronic's slot 0 with the existing shade ladder (`genre-shade.js`, `SHADE_OFFSETS`). No design-system change (ADR-0009 stays intact).
6. **iOS needs no change now.** The iOS app never decodes the vocabulary (it uses `GET /genres`, canonical names only, so `electronic` drops out). The Mac app hardcodes 11 families plus Other (`ios/MacSources`, GenData and StatData): keep `GET /genres/graph` on the 11 colour families through a form-to-colour-family mapping (BS).
7. **API additive** (API-117/119): `GET /genres/vocabulary` gets `groups`, row fields `assignable` and `groups`, a longer `families` list; `subfamilies` and `levels[g].subfamily` are served for one release, derived.
8. `subfamily` field and `subfamilies` block become redundant (the form is an ancestor); drop after the web is migrated.
9. `genre_discogs.json` (mirror) must agree with levels (`test_genre_payload_agrees`); `genre_extras.json` `edm` leaves the tree; `genre_sources.json` `is_family` must be true only for Discogs level 1 names (House, Techno, Trance are Discogs styles).
