# Setting: auto-jump to a percentage of the song to skip the intro

> Alex: add a config "autojump to xx% of the song", to have the possibility to skip the intro.

## Proposed behaviour

- A Settings option "Start songs at" with a percentage (0% = off, default off; for example 0-50%).
- When a song starts from the beginning, playback seeks to that share of its duration. Resuming a song, seeking by hand, and Previous (restart) do not jump again.
- Applies to the queue's automatic advance and to Play on a row; a song the user explicitly restarts keeps the manual position.
- Stored per user (web: settings store; iOS: app setting), no server contract change.

- B. Per-song override (remember a custom start) later?
- C. Short songs (under about 60 s): skip the jump?
