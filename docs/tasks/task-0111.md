# Studio Tools: sound level meter

> Alex (2026-10-24): step 1 is only the synth; "the rest: create follow-up tickets".

Follow-up of [task-0106](task-0106.md). Nothing is built.

## Scope

- Level in **dBFS**, exact (relative to digital full scale of the input; 0 dBFS is the maximum).
- **Approximate dB(A)**: A-weighting filter plus a calibration offset the user sets once against a reference sound level meter. Always labelled "approximate".
- Min, average and maximum since the last reset, and a peak hold with a Reset button.
- **dBm is not applicable** (it is electrical power relative to 1 mW), so the tool does not show it.
- Honest limits shown on the card: phone and laptop microphones are not calibrated for sound pressure level; without a calibration offset the dB(A) value can be 5 to 10 dB off. Not for safety, health or legal noise measurement.
- The microphone is opened without echo cancellation, noise suppression and automatic gain, otherwise the level means nothing.

## Open questions (recommendations, not decisions)

- A. Time weighting (Fast 125 ms, Slow 1 s)? Recommended: Fast, with a Slow toggle later.
- B. What is "average"? Recommended: the energy average (Leq) since reset.
- C. Where is the offset kept? Recommended: browser storage per device, with a "Calibrate" step that asks for the reference reading.
- D. Other weightings (C, Z)? Recommended: dBFS and dB(A) only.
