# Genre and date lookup rules for versions and albums

One ticket for the post-metadata build batch, after task-0036 ships. Requirements: TAG-088 to TAG-094 in [tags.md](../specs/tags.md#genres-for-versions-and-albums-decision-be1-with-bb1-and-bd-queued-in-task-0042); Decision AR in [metadata-model-decisions-AR.md](../tech/metadata-model-decisions-AR.md).

## Context

Lookups use title plus artist, so a version gets the original recording's genres and dates (Prodigy "Voodoo People (Pendulum Remix)" got techno and hardcore from the original plus drum and bass from Pendulum), and a recording matched only on a compilation gives a wrong year and country.

## Deliverables, in order

- [ ] (a) Version genre rule (BE1, TAG-088 to TAG-093): automatic genres only from a MusicBrainz or Discogs entry for the remix found by base title plus remixer, or a Wikipedia page about the remix; remixer's artist-level genres as marked suggestions; the original's genres as a hint only; otherwise empty; covers and live versions inherit the original's genres as suggestions only; typed or accepted genres win.
- [ ] (b) Album-first genre ranking (BD, TAG-094): Discogs release or master styles, then MusicBrainz release-group tags with at least 2 votes, then the Wikipedia song page, then artist-level genres as suggestions only.
- [ ] (c) Per-song `rejected_genres` guard (BB1, TAG-093): a rejected genre is never suggested again; stored with the song (data-model.md entry to add when built).
- [ ] (d) AS1 remix-specific date lookup: title plus remixer against MusicBrainz and Discogs for `version.year`; never the original's year, never the upload year when a specific source exists.
- [ ] (e) Release-group rule: when an album release group is known, take `released` and the country from its earliest release, not from a compilation recording. Example group: https://musicbrainz.org/release-group/b6c215ef-f876-3176-8290-9c1edbe8ad8e (Taylor Dayne "Tell It to My Heart", first release 1987, US). Wrong match to avoid: MusicBrainz recording 1ccdf4a6-383c-4fc3-a766-a8065d8df8eb, only on the 2001 German compilation "The Disco Boys, Volume 1".
- [ ] (f) The Decision AR code paths listed in [metadata-model-decisions-AR.md](../tech/metadata-model-decisions-AR.md#code-paths-that-must-change) (extractor `_year`, `lookup._apply_suggestions`, `metadata.merge`, `genre_lookup._first_year`, `discogs_lookup`, `downloads`, `metafill`, web form).

- [ ] (g) MusicBrainz artist-credit override (DATA-142): a known MusicBrainz (optionally Discogs) artist credit replaces the title-parsed main/featured split as a suggestion to confirm; credit order and join phrases preserved. Example: YouTube title "Gisele Bündchen - Heart of Glass (Official Video) ft. Bob Sinclar", MusicBrainz recording ab31247c-3a4f-40a0-b859-80d51721c390 credits "Gisele & Bob Sinclar", so `artists` = [Gisele Bündchen, Bob Sinclar], `featured` = [].
- [ ] (h) Cover detection from the MusicBrainz work (DATA-143): follow the recording's work relation; if the work's earliest known recording is by a different artist than the credited ones, suggest `version.kind = cover` and `version.of` = that artist (Heart of Glass is Blondie's 1979 song). Suggestion only; a title "(X Cover)" wins (ADD-126); no original recording id, album, year or genres attached. Uncertain cases: several earliest recordings, same-artist re-recording (not a cover), covers of covers, remix (not a cover).

## Test cases

- The Prodigy "Voodoo People (Pendulum Remix)": no techno or hardcore applied; "original: techno, hardcore" shown as a hint; drum and bass only as a Pendulum suggestion unless a remix-specific source exists.
- Haddaway "What Is Love" (1993): `released` 1993, never the 2002 or 2003 compilation years.
- Taylor Dayne "Tell It to My Heart": `released` 1987 and country US from the release group's earliest release, not 2001 and Germany.
- A genre typed, accepted or rejected by the user survives every lookup and backfill.
- Gisele Bündchen / Bob Sinclar "Heart of Glass": suggested `artists` = [Gisele Bündchen, Bob Sinclar], `featured` = []; and a suggested cover of Blondie (work earliest recording 1979); neither applied without confirmation.
- Teemid "Crazy (Gnarls Barkley Cover)": the title rule (ADD-126) wins, `version.of` = Gnarls Barkley, no work-based suggestion overrides it.
- A same-artist re-recording (work's earliest recording by the same credited artist): no cover suggestion.
- A song with no work relation: no cover suggestion.

## Open questions

- A. Is a Discogs entry for the remix matched by remixer name alone enough, or must the title match exactly?
- B. Does a live version get the original's genres as chips in the Suggested row, or only as the hint?
- C. Where is `rejected_genres` shown or reset in the UI?
- D. Re-run on existing songs, or only on new lookups (Decision AR: no automatic re-dating)?
- E. Which other sources may confirm a cover or credit: Discogs, the Wikipedia song page ("originally recorded by")?
- F. What confidence threshold is needed before showing the artist-credit or cover suggestion (e.g. a single clear earliest recording, title/artist match score)?
- G. Where in the song form do these suggestions show (next to the Artists/Featuring pills, and the Version section)?
