Remaining: a held note with a Clear button, the inharmonicity estimate, the keyboard or tuner display (needle in cents).

## Goal

A tool in the Analyze group of the Studio site (a page beside Live BPM, `#/analyze/...`) that listens to the microphone and shows the note being played or sung.

## Scope

- Pitch name and octave (for example A3), the frequency in Hz, and the deviation in cents with a needle or bar.
- Harmonic peaks: the strongest peaks of the spectrum near n times the fundamental, listed as H1, H2, ... with Hz and dB (same labels as the lab).
- Inharmonicity estimate: fit `f_n = n f0 sqrt(1 + B n^2)` to the measured peaks and show B (a string or bell is stretched, a clean synth is B = 0).
- Hold: keep the last stable note on screen when the sound stops, with a visible "held" state and a way to clear it.
- Pitch from YIN or autocorrelation on the time signal; the harmonic peaks from the spectrum.

## Open questions (recommendations, not decisions)

- A. Algorithm? Recommended: YIN (fewer octave errors than plain autocorrelation), with a pure function that is unit-tested on synthesised signals.
- B. Detection range? Recommended: fundamentals from about 50 Hz to 2 kHz; outside it shows "out of range".
- C. Hold length? Recommended: hold until the next stable note, plus a Clear button.
- D. A4 reference? Recommended: 440 Hz until [task-0109](task-0109.md) adds the setting.
- E. Noisy input: show nothing below a confidence threshold. Recommended: yes, a single "no clear pitch" state.
