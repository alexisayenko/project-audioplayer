# Studio Tools: waveforms page

> Alex (decision HQ3, 2026-10-25): the waveform entries (sawtooth, square, triangle, pulse, noise) go into the Glossary now; the page that lets you hear and see them is a ticket, not built yet.

Follow-up of [task-0106](task-0106.md) (harmonics lab) and [task-0114](task-0114.md) (the site); site: [studio-site.md](../specs/studio-site.md). The standalone waveforms page with pulse and noise is not built. Built so far: the Glossary has the terms (Sawtooth wave, Square wave, Triangle wave, Pulse wave, Duty cycle, White noise, Pink noise, Fourier transform, Spectrum analyzer), and the Glossary cone animation has a waveform switch (sine, triangle, square, sawtooth); the Primitive page still plays a single sine wave.

## What the page shows

- A wave choice: sine, triangle, square, sawtooth, pulse (with a duty-cycle control), white noise, pink noise.
- The waveform on the oscilloscope and the spectrum next to it, so you see why a square wave has only odd harmonics (amplitude 1/n), a sawtooth has all of them (1/n) and a triangle has odd ones that fall as 1/n squared.
- The harmonic list as in the lab (level of H1 to Hn), for the periodic waves.
- Frequency and level controls as on the Primitive page.

## Relations

- Reuses the synth, the oscilloscope and the spectrum view of the harmonics lab ([task-0106](task-0106.md)); the periodic waves can be built as a sum of the lab's harmonics with the amplitudes above, or with the browser's oscillator types.
- Noise needs a buffer source (white) and a filter (pink, minus 3 dB per octave), not an oscillator.
- The terms and map links already exist in the Glossary (see the waveform and noise relations in `web/js/glossary-map.js`).

## Open questions (recommendations, not decisions)

- A. Where does it live in the menu? Recommended: a new item "Waveforms" in the Synthesize group, above Chordophones, since it is the simplest source after Primitive.
- B. Band-limited or naive oscillators? A naive sawtooth or square aliases at high pitch. Recommended: the browser's built-in oscillator types (band-limited) for sound, and the exact harmonic sum for the picture.
- C. One page or part of Primitive? Recommended: a separate page; Primitive stays a single sine ("H1 only").
- D. Pulse width range? Recommended: 5 to 95 percent in 1 percent steps, default 50 (a square wave).
- E. Pink noise method? Recommended: a pinking filter on white noise (three-pole approximation), no random-walk tricks; show it as a fall of 3 dB per octave on the log-frequency spectrum.
- F. Loudness safety: noise and square waves are much louder than a sine at the same peak. Recommended: a lower default level and the same limiter as the other synth pages.
