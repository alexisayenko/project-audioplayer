## Goal

Let the waveform, spectrum and spectrogram views of the harmonics lab show more than the synth.

## Scope

- A source switch **Synth / Microphone / Player** above the three views.
- Synth: the lab's own signal (step 1).
- Microphone: the live input (`echoCancellation`, `noiseSuppression` and `autoGainControl` off). Its signal is analysed only and never sent to the speakers (no feedback); permission is asked when "Listen" is tapped. Stops on Stop, a source or route change, a hidden page, 10 minutes, a lost track or a device change.
- Player: the song that is playing, read from the visualizers' shared analyser (`audio-graph.js`, VIZ-001 to VIZ-005; same origin, so no CORS silencing). The card builds the graph on demand through `unlock()` and never creates a second `MediaElementAudioSourceNode`. Playback is not changed. "Nothing is playing" when idle.
- One small source interface (an `AnalyserNode` plus a sample rate): the views do not care where the signal comes from. The synth controls apply to Synth only and are hidden for the other sources with one explanatory line.
- Live signals have no Hn: Linear axis 0 to 5 kHz (2, 5 or 10 kHz selectable), Log 20 Hz to 20 kHz, the real analyser curve, a triggered waveform, a peak readout ("Peak: 220 Hz (A3 +0 cents)").

## Relations

- Audio graph and analyser rules: [visualizer.md](../specs/visualizer.md). Its graph is created lazily on a user gesture and the analyser exists only while the context runs.
- Reused by the [note detector](task-0107.md) and the [spectrum analyzer tool](task-0112.md).
