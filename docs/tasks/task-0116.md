## Physics

- Sound comes from a stretched skin.
- The partials are not whole multiples of the base frequency. For an ideal circular membrane the ratios are 1, 1.59, 2.14, 2.30, 2.65 and 2.92 (zeros of Bessel functions).
- So drums have a less clear pitch than strings or pipes.

## Controls to build

- Base frequency.
- Strike position (a strike near the centre excites the symmetric modes strongly; near the rim, more modes).
- Damping per partial (higher partials fade faster).
- Tension.
- Kettledrum pitch tuning (a kettledrum is tuned so that a partial, usually the second, lands on a chosen note).

- C. Strike position as a number or as a picture of the head? Recommended: a slider from centre to rim; amplitude per mode from the Bessel function at that radius.
